Privacy Policy

Effective Date: 25 November 2026 · Publisher: Ogro Labs · Contact: support@shazid.me

BornoDesk is local-first by design. This policy explains — in plain language — what the Android app and the Windows companion app do with your data, and what they never do.

1.Our promise: zero cloud

BornoDesk connects your Android phone directly to your own Windows PC. Your keystrokes, touchpad movements, clipboard text and transferred files travel ONLY between your phone and your PC — over your local Wi-Fi, USB cable or Bluetooth — and the connection is encrypted.

Nothing is ever uploaded to any external server. We run no cloud service, keep no account system, and store no copy of your keystrokes, files or clipboard on our side. There is nothing for us to leak, sell or lose.

2.Permissions we use — and why

BornoDesk asks for only the permissions it needs to do its job. It never asks for location, contacts, microphone, SMS or storage access beyond what is listed here.

  • Camera — QR pairing only. Used only to scan the QR code shown on your PC screen so your phone and PC can pair. No photos or videos are taken or stored.
  • Local Network / Wi-Fi / Bluetooth / USB — connecting to your PC. Used only to find and connect to your own PC on your local network, cable or Bluetooth link. No data leaves your local connection.
  • Notifications — keeping your connection alive. Used only to show that BornoDesk is connected and to keep the active connection alive through a foreground service while you use it.

3.How the free allowance is measured — without accounts

The Android app gives you 30 minutes of active connected use free every day, and a short rewarded video unlocks 24 hours of unlimited use. To measure this fairly without asking you to create an account, the Android app computes a one-way SHA-256 hash of your device's ANDROID_ID and shares it only with your paired Windows PC, over the local connection.

  • The hash cannot be reversed back into your ANDROID_ID, and it is never sent to any external server.
  • It is used only to track your daily 30-minute free allowance and your 24-hour passes on your own devices.
  • Clearing a pairing or uninstalling the app removes it.

4.Advertising — Android app only

The Android app shows ads to keep the Windows companion app 100% free. The Windows companion app contains zero ads and zero trackers.

  • Google AdMob. The Android app uses Google AdMob for a Home banner ad and optional rewarded video ads. Watching one short rewarded video unlocks 24 hours of unlimited use and hides the Home banner ad.
  • Consent. Where required by law, Google's UMP (User Messaging Platform) asks for your consent before personalised ads are shown.
  • What AdMob may collect. AdMob may collect standard advertising identifiers and related device information to serve and measure ads, as described in Google's Privacy Policy.

Learn more: Google Privacy Policy · AdMob & Privacy

5.Children's privacy

BornoDesk is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. BornoDesk collects no account information from anyone — adult or child — because it has no accounts at all. If you believe a child has shared personal information through an ad shown in the app, please contact us at support@shazid.me and we will help.

6.Your control

You are in control of your data at all times:

  • Unpair a PC anytime from the Android app — the pairing and its local records are removed.
  • Clear the app's data from your Android settings to remove everything the app stored on your phone.
  • Uninstall the Android app or the Windows companion app anytime — nothing of yours stays on our side, because nothing was ever sent to us.
  • Turn off personalised ads from your Android settings (Settings → Privacy → Ads) or through Google's ad settings.

7.Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we will update the Effective Date above and publish the new version on this page. If a change affects how your data is handled, we will highlight it in the app as well.
