Install & pair in 3 steps

From download to typing on your PC — about a minute.

1

Run the installer

Get BornoDesk from the Microsoft Store — or download the .exe directly. It installs in seconds and shows a QR code on your PC screen.

2

Open the Android app

Open BornoDesk on your phone and tap “Pair a PC”. Your PC appears automatically on the same Wi-Fi.

3

Scan the QR

Point your camera at the QR on your PC. Paired — your phone is now your PC's keyboard, touchpad & gamepad.

About the Windows SmartScreen prompt: If you install from the direct .exe download, Windows may show a SmartScreen message because BornoDesk is a new app. Choose “More info” → “Run anyway”. BornoDesk needs no account, and your phone and PC talk only to each other over an encrypted local connection — nothing is uploaded anywhere.
